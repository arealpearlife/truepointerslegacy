# Legacy and Flawless catalog contract

## Brand hierarchy

- Brand: True Pointers
- Collection: Legacy
- Hero product: True Pointers Legacy Extended Tennis Chain
- Premium product: True Pointers Flawless Extended Tennis Chain

Legacy is never labeled Standard, budget, basic, or inferior. Flawless is an aspirational premium alternative. Customer-facing titles and primary marketing do not use CZ or moissanite, but accurate material disclosures must remain available before purchase.

## Shopify authority

Shopify is authoritative for product and variant IDs, prices, media, inventory, availability, checkout, orders, refunds, and fulfillment.

Both products use:

- Option 1: Stone size — 3 mm, 4 mm, 5 mm
- Option 2: Chain length — 40 inches, 44 inches, 48 inches

Every combination is a distinct Shopify variant. SKUs, inventory quantities, weights, and physical availability remain unset until the owner verifies them. Products remain draft and unavailable to purchase until those values and genuine media are approved.

## Storefront behavior

- Lead every principal shopping journey with Legacy.
- Use `EXPLORE FLAWLESS` as the secondary invitation.
- The Flawless modal never replaces a Legacy selection automatically.
- No product is added to cart without explicit customer selection.
- Exact variant matching is required; no closest-option substitution.
- Display price and availability from the current Shopify variant.
- Unavailable variants remain visible only when the experience clearly marks and disables them.
- Genuine media is managed in Shopify Admin; placeholders must identify themselves as placeholders.

## Required disclosures and missing production data

Before publication, the owner must verify:

- exact metal composition, plating, clasp, construction, and stone details;
- variant SKUs;
- inventory quantities and selling policy;
- product and shipping weights;
- fulfillment location and processing time;
- genuine Legacy and Flawless images/video with alt text;
- care, warranty, returns, refund, shipping, privacy, and terms content.

No unsupported claims about diamonds, grading, testing, durability, allergies, plating life, endorsements, scarcity, or customer reviews may be published.
