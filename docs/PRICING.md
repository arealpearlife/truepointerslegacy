# Pricing and cost planning

Do not place customer-specific quotes, credentials, or confidential contracts in this tracked file.

Estimate and approve:

- Payment processing rates and chargeback fees
- Card readers, tablets, printers, scanners, drawers, stands, and replacements
- Apple Developer Program and Mac/cloud-Mac access
- Hosting, database, object storage, backups, monitoring, email, and SMS
- Tax, accounting, inventory, and ecommerce integrations
- Design, engineering, testing, support, training, and maintenance
- Security review, legal review, insurance, and compliance work

Store confidential vendor quotes under `private/`, where Git ignores them.
